## A Nurturing Start for Your Little One

Our infant care program is designed to provide the gentle, responsive care your baby needs during their first year of life. We understand that choosing childcare for your infant is one of the most important decisions you'll make, and we're committed to providing a safe, loving environment where your baby can thrive.

### What Your Baby Will Experience

Our experienced caregivers focus on building secure attachments with each infant while supporting their rapid development through:

- Responsive caregiving that follows your baby's individual cues and schedule
- Sensory exploration activities including tummy time, music, and age-appropriate toys
- Language development through talking, singing, and reading throughout the day
- Safe, clean play areas designed specifically for infants
- Daily communication with parents about feedings, diaper changes, and milestones

### Our Approach to Infant Care

We believe in creating a home-like environment where infants feel secure and loved. Our caregivers:

- Hold, rock, and comfort babies throughout the day
- Maintain consistent routines that help infants feel secure
- Support developmental milestones through age-appropriate activities
- Practice safe sleep guidelines recommended by pediatricians
- Communicate regularly with parents about their baby's day

### Health and Safety

Your baby's health and safety are our top priorities:

- All caregivers are trained in infant CPR and first aid
- We follow strict hygiene protocols including frequent hand washing and sanitizing
- Our infant room is equipped with safe cribs, changing stations, and age-appropriate toys
- We maintain temperature control and proper ventilation
- Regular health screenings and immunization requirements

### Daily Schedule Sample

While we follow each infant's individual rhythm, a typical day might include:

- **Morning**: Arrival, free play, bottles/feeding, diaper changes
- **Mid-Morning**: Tummy time, sensory activities, outdoor time (weather permitting)
- **Lunch**: Bottles/feeding, diaper changes
- **Afternoon**: Naptime in cribs, quiet play upon waking
- **Late Afternoon**: Outdoor play, music time, bottles/feeding
- **Evening**: Calm activities, parent pick-up with daily report
