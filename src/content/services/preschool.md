## Building the Foundation for Lifelong Learning

Our preschool program prepares children for kindergarten and beyond through a comprehensive curriculum that balances academic readiness with social-emotional development and creative expression.

### Curriculum Highlights

Our STEAM-based approach integrates:

**Literacy Development**
- Phonics and letter recognition
- Storytelling and comprehension
- Early writing skills
- Library time and book exploration

**Mathematics**
- Number recognition and counting
- Patterns, sorting, and classification
- Measurement and comparison
- Problem-solving activities

**Science & Discovery**
- Hands-on experiments
- Nature exploration
- Simple machines and engineering
- Weather and seasons

**Arts & Creativity**
- Drawing, painting, and sculpting
- Music and movement
- Dramatic play and theater
- Process-focused art experiences

**Social-Emotional Learning**
- Friendship skills and cooperation
- Emotion recognition and regulation
- Conflict resolution
- Independence and responsibility

### School Readiness Skills

We focus on preparing children for kindergarten success:

- Following multi-step directions
- Working independently and in groups
- Sitting for group instruction
- Managing personal belongings
- Problem-solving and critical thinking
- Self-regulation and focus

### Daily Schedule

- **7:00-8:30 AM**: Arrival, breakfast, morning centers
- **8:30-9:00 AM**: Circle time, calendar, morning meeting
- **9:00-10:00 AM**: Literacy and language arts
- **10:00-10:30 AM**: Snack and outdoor play
- **10:30-11:30 AM**: STEAM activities and projects
- **11:30-12:00 PM**: Lunch
- **12:00-2:30 PM**: Rest/quiet time (Pre-K may have shorter rest)
- **2:30-3:00 PM**: Snack
- **3:00-4:00 PM**: Centers (dramatic play, blocks, art, science)
- **4:00-5:00 PM**: Outdoor play
- **5:00-6:00 PM**: Free choice and departure

### Learning Through Play

We believe children learn best through play-based experiences:

- Theme-based units that integrate multiple subject areas
- Learning centers where children explore at their own pace
- Project-based learning that develops inquiry skills
- Outdoor education connecting children with nature
- Technology integration with educational apps and tools

### Family Engagement

Parents are partners in their child's education:

- Monthly curriculum newsletters
- Parent-teacher conferences twice per year
- Family events and celebrations
- Portfolio updates showing your child's progress
- Daily communication through our parent app

### Kindergarten Transition

In our Pre-K year, we focus on:

- School routines and expectations
- Academic skills assessment
- Kindergarten readiness portfolio
- Transition activities and school visits
- Parent information sessions
