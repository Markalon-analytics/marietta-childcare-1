## Where Curiosity Comes Alive

The toddler years are filled with discovery, independence, and rapid development. Our toddler program provides a safe, nurturing environment where your little one can explore, learn, and grow at their own pace.

### What Your Toddler Will Learn

Our curriculum supports the whole child through:

- **Language Development**: Vocabulary building through songs, stories, and conversations
- **Social Skills**: Sharing, taking turns, and playing cooperatively with peers
- **Motor Skills**: Climbing, running, stacking, and fine motor activities
- **Emotional Growth**: Identifying feelings and developing self-regulation
- **Independence**: Self-help skills like feeding, washing hands, and cleanup

### Daily Activities

Each day is filled with age-appropriate activities designed to engage toddlers:

- Circle time with songs, movement, and stories
- Sensory play with sand, water, playdough, and art materials
- Outdoor play for gross motor development
- Music and movement activities
- Dramatic play and pretend scenarios
- Building with blocks and manipulatives

### Our Teaching Approach

We understand that toddlers learn best through:

- **Play-Based Learning**: Activities that feel like play but build essential skills
- **Routine and Structure**: Consistent schedules that help toddlers feel secure
- **Positive Guidance**: Teaching appropriate behavior through modeling and redirection
- **Individual Attention**: Small group sizes with a 1:6 teacher-to-child ratio
- **Family Partnership**: Regular communication about your child's progress

### Typical Daily Schedule

- **7:00-8:30 AM**: Arrival, free play, breakfast
- **8:30-9:00 AM**: Circle time and morning activities
- **9:00-10:30 AM**: Centers (art, blocks, dramatic play, sensory)
- **10:30-11:00 AM**: Outdoor play
- **11:00-11:30 AM**: Lunch
- **11:30-2:30 PM**: Rest time
- **2:30-3:00 PM**: Snack
- **3:00-4:30 PM**: Learning centers and activities
- **4:30-6:00 PM**: Outdoor play and free choice

### Nutrition and Meals

We provide nutritious meals and snacks that introduce toddlers to a variety of healthy foods. Our menus are designed with toddler nutrition in mind and accommodate dietary restrictions.
