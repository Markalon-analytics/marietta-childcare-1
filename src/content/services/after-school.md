## Safe, Fun Care After the School Bell Rings

Our after-school program provides school-age children with a safe, engaging environment where they can relax, complete homework, and enjoy enriching activities with friends.

### What Makes Our Program Special

**Homework Support**
- Dedicated homework time with staff assistance
- Quiet study areas
- Help with organization and time management
- Communication with parents about homework completion

**Enrichment Activities**
- STEM projects and experiments
- Arts and crafts
- Sports and physical activities
- Cooking and life skills
- Technology and coding clubs
- Drama and performing arts

**Social Development**
- Building friendships across age groups
- Leadership opportunities
- Team-building activities
- Conflict resolution skills

### Daily Schedule

**After School (3:00-6:00 PM)**
- Arrival and snack
- Homework time (30-45 minutes)
- Outdoor recreation
- Enrichment activities and clubs
- Free choice time

**School Holidays & Breaks**
- Field trips to museums, parks, and attractions
- Special theme days and projects
- Extended outdoor time
- Guest speakers and workshops
- Movie days and celebrations

### Safe Transportation

We provide safe transportation from participating elementary schools:

- Background-checked drivers
- Secure vehicles with appropriate restraints
- Daily attendance tracking
- Direct communication with parents

### Age-Appropriate Activities

We tailor activities to different age groups:

**Elementary (6-8 years)**
- Structured games and sports
- Creative arts and crafts
- Science exploration
- Group projects

**Pre-Teens (9-12 years)**
- Independent project time
- Technology and robotics
- Sports leagues
- Leadership roles as helpers

### Flexible Scheduling

We understand school schedules vary:

- Part-time or full-time enrollment
- Drop-in care available (space permitting)
- Early release day coverage
- School break camps
- Summer program

### Healthy Snacks

We provide nutritious after-school snacks:

- Fresh fruits and vegetables
- Whole grain options
- Dairy and protein choices
- Accommodations for allergies and dietary needs

### Character Development

Our program emphasizes:

- Responsibility and independence
- Kindness and respect
- Teamwork and cooperation
- Problem-solving skills
- Academic motivation

### Parent Communication

Stay connected to your child's experience:

- Daily sign-in/sign-out system
- Monthly activity calendars
- Photo updates of special activities
- Progress reports on homework habits
- Open door policy for parent visits
