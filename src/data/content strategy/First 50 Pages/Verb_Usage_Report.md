## 3. Phase-Based Verb Bank

### A. PROBLEM PHASE VERBS
- **Core set:** need, seek, want, desire, struggle, worry, concern, fear, uncertain
- **Typical patterns:** 
  - "Parents **need** quality childcare they can **trust**."
  - "Working parents **struggle** to find affordable, reliable care."
  - "Many families **worry** about their child's safety and development."

### B. SOLUTION PHASE VERBS
- **Core set:** provide, offer, deliver, create, support, ensure, protect, nurture
- **Typical patterns:**
  - "We **provide** quality childcare that **supports** your family."
  - "Our programs **create** safe, nurturing environments for learning."
  - "We **ensure** your child receives individualized attention and care."

### C. PROCESS PHASE VERBS
- **Core set:** care, engage, guide, encourage, explore, teach, learn, develop
- **Typical patterns:**
  - "We **care** for your child with experienced, certified educators."
  - "Children **engage** in play-based learning activities daily."
  - "Our educators **guide** each child's development at their own pace."

### D. OUTCOME PHASE VERBS
- **Core set:** nurture, develop, thrive, build, prepare, achieve, grow, empower, inspire
- **Typical patterns:**
  - "**Nurture** your child's potential in our safe, loving environment."
  - "Watch your child **thrive** and **grow** with us."
  - "We **prepare** children for kindergarten and lifelong learning success."

## 5. Industry-Specific Verb Layer

**Domain-Specific Verbs for Childcare:**

- **Nurture** – Provide loving, attentive care that supports emotional and physical growth
  - Example: "**Nurture** your child's development in our warm, supportive environment."

- **Develop** – Foster growth in cognitive, social, emotional, and physical domains
  - Example: "**Develop** essential skills through play-based learning activities."

- **Foster** – Encourage and promote specific developmental outcomes and learning
  - Example: "**Foster** independence, creativity, and confidence in every child."

## 6. Integration Guidelines

### Intro / Problem Section (first 100–150 words)
- Include 2–4 core **Problem-phase** verbs: need, seek, worry, struggle
- Goal: Surface parent's concerns and needs using their language
- Example: "Parents **need** quality childcare they can **trust**. You **worry** about your child's safety and development. You **seek** educators who truly **care**."

### Solution Overview Section
- Lean on **Solution-phase** verbs: provide, support, ensure, protect, nurture
- Frame your offer as: "We **provide** quality childcare that **supports** your family and **ensures** your child's safety and growth."

### Process / How-to Section
- Pick 1–2 verb progression patterns as the spine of H2/H3 structure
- Recommended patterns: Pattern 1 (nurture → develop → prepare → achieve → thrive) and Pattern 3 (engage → explore → learn → grow → inspire)
- Ensure each step appears in headings, lists, and explanatory paragraphs

### Outcome / Benefits Section
- Use **Outcome-phase** verbs: nurture, develop, thrive, prepare, achieve, build, empower
- Tie each verb to specific, tangible benefits
- Example: "**Nurture** your child's potential. **Develop** essential skills. **Prepare** for kindergarten. **Achieve** milestones. **Thrive** in our community."
